Club & governance

Founded in 1975, Griffith Kart Club Inc. is an incorporated association in NSW and a member club of Karting Australia (NSW).

Our constitution

The club operates under the NSW Fair Trading Model Constitution for incorporated associations, made under the Associations Incorporation Act 2009 (NSW) and the Associations Incorporation Regulation 2022. When NSW Fair Trading updates the Model Constitution, the changes apply to our club automatically.

  • Sets out how members join, their rights, and how membership ends.
  • Covers the committee: office bearers, elections, meetings and quorum.
  • Governs the AGM, general meetings, voting and special resolutions.
  • Requires proper records, a register of members and annual financial reporting.
